      How it works

      1. 1Pick two molecules from the lists, or tap an example.
      2. 2Press Find out. The Finder asks four yes/no questions about them.
      3. 3Read the verdict: constitutional isomers, cis/trans isomers, enantiomers, the same molecule, or not isomers.
      4. 4Molecules on paper? Open “Your own molecules” and answer the questions yourself.
