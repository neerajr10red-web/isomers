How it works

  1. 1Drag a model to turn it. Double-click resets it, and Ctrl + scroll zooms.
  2. 2Explore shows every isomer of a formula, all drawn to the same scale.
  3. 3Spin the Bond lets you twist single, double and triple bonds.
  4. 4Mirror Challenge: line your molecule up with the dashed target. Round 2 is the mirror image.
