Isomers by Neeraj the Beloved

“What is an isomer, oh great Neeraj?”

Molecules with the same formula but a different arrangement of atoms. Both of these are C₄H₁₀, but one is a straight chain and the other is branched. Drag them to turn them around.

Try it yourself

  • 3D Lab: see every isomer of a formula, twist single and double bonds, and try to stack a molecule on its mirror image.
  • Isomer Finder: pick two molecules and find out what kind of isomers they are.
